Proposed trading principles

Simulated execution

Virtual orders record entry, exit, volume, stop loss and take profit.

Risk first

Show estimated loss at stop-loss before confirming a demo order. Apply account-risk limits.

Clear ETB records

Keep virtual balances separate from any real deposits. This prototype moves no money.

Regulatory condition: Get Ethiopian legal advice and written regulatory classification before accepting deposits or promising cash payouts. A simulated interface can still raise regulatory and customer-fund issues when real money is involved.
